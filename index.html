About

From operating complex systems to exploring AI-assisted decision systems.

My background is in large-scale execution, business transformation, and analytical decision-making. Over time, I developed a strong interest in AI not only as a technology trend, but as a practical tool for improving clarity, speed, and judgment in information-heavy environments.

This site is a small home for that work: selected projects, notes, and parts of an ongoing learning journey in machine learning, deep learning, and applied AI for business.
